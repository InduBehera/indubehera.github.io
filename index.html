AI-Enabled Content Generation for HCP Communication

From approved SmPC and messaging logic to compliant marketing content, at scale

The Problem

Pharma marketing teams couldn't produce compliant content fast enough. Off-the-shelf AI tools didn't understand review constraints, approved source material, or the logic of regulated pharmaceutical communication. Every content cycle was a bottleneck.

What I Did

Worked end-to-end with the tech team, from project scoping and PRD writing through to GTM launch. Defined the content workflow architecture, the messaging logic layer, and the SmPC-to-content generation pipeline. Led stakeholder enablement, sales narrative development, and commercial launch positioning for medical affairs and marketing audiences.

What was built

  • AI-enabled content generation platform using approved source material
  • Generates RTE content, HCP emails, and modular campaign assets from approved product SmPC
  • Review-aware prompt frameworks with messaging logic layer
  • Modular content block architecture for campaign deployment
  • Medical-marketing alignment workflow built into the system

Business value delivered

Compliant content in minutes, not days
Consistent messaging across all channels
Reduced review cycle dependency
Medical-marketing alignment built in

  • Evidence gap detection and landscape mapping logic
  • Insight extraction and structured summarisation layer
  • One-click conversion of insights to communication assets
  • Automatic competitor card generation from publication signals

USE CASE 04

AI-Enabled Forecasting Platform

From bespoke Excel forecasts to a governed, signal-driven workspace

The Problem

Forecasting teams rebuilt patient-based epidemiological models from scratch in Excel for every asset, market, and indication. Slow, inconsistent, hard to audit, impossible to compare across a portfolio. Erosion and risk surfaced late, buried in tabs.

What I Did

Translated forecasting-lead requirements into an interactive prototype and proposal. Defined the two-builder architecture (epidemiology bottom-up plus market top-down), a live model-flow builder that generates formula-wired Excel templates, a portfolio Model Library, and a signal-to-explanation AI layer that flags rule-based risks without prescribing. Kept Excel as the calculation engine of record. Owned the GTM narrative and enablement assets.

What was built

  • Two-builder architecture: epidemiology bottom-up plus market top-down
  • Live model-flow builder generating formula-wired Excel templates
  • Portfolio Model Library with standardised methodology
  • Signal-to-explanation AI layer flagging rule-based risks
  • Governance layer with audit trail and version control

Business value delivered

Model builds collapsed from days to a guided wizard
Standardised methodology across the portfolio
Erosion velocity and scenario divergence surface as signals
Risks found, not hunted for

  • Country and brand movement interpretation with growth momentum scoring
  • Competitor share shift alerts and rank-gap interpretation
  • Rep visibility and territory activity summarisation
  • Leadership quick takes and recommended action prompts

USE CASE 06

KOLens — KOL Intelligence Workspace

From fragmented KOL signals to a ranked, searchable influence network

The Problem

Medical affairs teams spend weeks manually mapping KOL influence across therapy areas. Signals sit scattered in publications, conference presentations, trial investigators, guidelines, and social. No unified view. No ranking logic. Every launch team rebuilds the map from scratch.

What I Did

Built a live prototype from scoping to shipped demo. Defined the multi-source signal architecture (publications, congresses, trials, guidelines, social), the KOL ranking logic, the network visualisation, and the search and filter workflow. Owned the positioning, buyer narrative, and enablement story for medical affairs and commercial launch teams.

What was built

  • Multi-source signal ingestion: publications, congresses, trials, guidelines, social
  • KOL ranking algorithm surfacing influence beyond publication count
  • Searchable, filterable network map by therapy area and geography
  • Profile-level view: activity, affiliations, network position, engagement history
  • Live shippable prototype used in commercial buyer conversations

Business value delivered

Launch KOL mapping collapsed from weeks to hours
Influence ranked by signal, not publication volume
Medical affairs and commercial share one source of truth
Live demo shippable in buyer conversations
