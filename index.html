USE CASE 01

Ariya Compass & Content Wizard

From approved SmPC and messaging logic to compliant marketing content — at scale

The Problem

Pharma marketing teams couldn't produce compliant content fast enough. Off-the-shelf AI tools didn't understand review constraints, approved source material, or the logic of regulated pharmaceutical communication. Every content cycle was a bottleneck.

What I Did

Worked end-to-end with the tech team — from project scoping and PRD writing through to GTM launch. Defined the content workflow architecture, the messaging logic layer, and the SmPC-to-content generation pipeline. Led stakeholder enablement, sales narrative development, and commercial launch positioning for medical affairs and marketing audiences.

What was built

  • Ariya Compass — AI content creation platform using approved source material
  • Content Wizard — generates RTE content and marketing emails from product SmPC
  • Review-aware prompt frameworks with messaging logic layer
  • Modular content block architecture for campaign deployment
  • Medical-marketing alignment workflow built into the system

Business value delivered

Compliant content in minutes, not days
Consistent messaging across all channels
Reduced review cycle dependency
Medical-marketing alignment built in
USE CASE 02

MSL Sales Intelligence App

From Databricks data to daily WhatsApp field briefings — MSL intelligence, automated

The Problem

Medical Science Liaisons were walking into HCP visits under-prepared. They didn't know what was discussed last quarter, which channel the HCP preferred, or where their own performance gaps were. CRM data existed but wasn't being activated at the point of need.

What I Did

Scoped and executed end-to-end with the tech team. Defined the KPI architecture, the Databricks data pull logic, the prep note structure, and the WhatsApp notification channel design. Owned the GTM narrative, sales enablement story, and commercial launch positioning for medical affairs audiences.

What was built

  • Field intelligence app pulling live data from Databricks
  • KPI analysis engine surfacing performance gaps by territory
  • Personalised HCP prep notes generated before every visit
  • Activity summary from last quarter per HCP relationship
  • Daily sales brief delivered automatically via WhatsApp

Business value delivered

MSLs arrive prepared — every visit, every time
Performance gaps visible before they compound
Field readiness transformed from manual to intelligent
Delivered in the channel reps actually use — WhatsApp
USE CASE 03

Publications AI Engine

From publication overload to evidence clarity — query, gap-detect, and convert to competitor cards

The Problem

Pharma medical affairs teams had hundreds of publications but no structured way to query them. Evidence gaps went undetected. Insights sat in PDFs. Field teams re-commissioned the same research repeatedly because no one could access what already existed. The problem wasn't a lack of data — it was a lack of activation.

What I Did

Scoped and executed end-to-end with the tech team. Translated medical affairs requirements into PRDs defining the query architecture, evidence gap detection logic, and the insight-to-competitor-card conversion workflow. Owned the GTM narrative, target audience positioning for medical affairs and evidence teams, and all sales enablement assets for commercial launch.

What was built

  • Natural language query engine across full publications library
  • Evidence gap detection and landscape mapping logic
  • Insight extraction and structured summarisation layer
  • One-click conversion of insights to communication assets
  • Automatic competitor card generation from publication signals

Business value delivered

Evidence gaps found — not just searched for
Duplicate research spend eliminated
Insights converted to field assets automatically
Competitive intelligence built from publication signals
